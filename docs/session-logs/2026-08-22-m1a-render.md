## Design decisions worth remembering

1. **Adapter pattern keeps the renderer sync and dependency-light** — shiki/mermaid are
   initialised by the app and handed in; conformance suites omit them.
2. **Sanitisation boundary preserved**: KaTeX hydration happens strictly after DOMPurify;
   KaTeX needs inline styles + MathML that the allowlist must keep forbidding for
   document content. Same reason token colours are classes, not styles.
3. **Conformance byte-exactness untouched**: M3/M4 suites run through the raw path where
   anchors/highlighting are off; verified — engine suite still 57, CommonMark 652/652.
